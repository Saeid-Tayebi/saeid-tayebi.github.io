INDUSTRIAL MODELING

Recognizing changes before quality deteriorates

Industrial quality monitoring requires more than predicting individual failures. Patterns across successive observations can reveal changes that deserve investigation.

My work combines industrial data preparation, temporal modeling, and warning evaluation, including the burden of false alarms during healthy periods.

MECHANISTIC SIMULATION

Exploring reactor behavior through an engineering interface

For Polymerization Studio, I developed reactor models and a MATLAB interface for exploring operating conditions and polymer properties across configurable reactor arrangements.
